Vacation Packages

Each Aloha Horizon package includes round-trip airfare from Los Angeles, all hotel accommodations, daily breakfast, and 24/7 concierge support. Choose the adventure that speaks to your heart — or contact us to build a fully custom itinerary.

Oahu Explorer — 7 Nights

The Oahu Explorer is our most popular package for first-time Hawaii visitors. You will stay steps from the legendary Waikiki Beach in the heart of Honolulu, with easy access to the island's top attractions. Hike Diamond Head for panoramic ocean views, pay your respects at the Pearl Harbor National Memorial, explore the North Shore's world-famous surf breaks, and enjoy Honolulu's vibrant dining and nightlife scene.

Destination

Waikiki, Oahu — the iconic heart of Hawaiian tourism, blending golden beach with world-class hospitality.

Hotel Accommodations

Hilton Hawaiian Village Waikiki Beach Resort — 7 nights, ocean-view room included. One of Waikiki's landmark resort complexes with 5 pools, 20 restaurants, and direct beach access.

Rental Car

Alamo Rent A Car — Compact SUV for 7 days, perfect for exploring Oahu's coast and valleys at your own pace.

From $2,499 per person

Maui Romance — 5 Nights

Designed for couples, the Maui Romance package delivers the ultimate in Hawaiian luxury and intimacy. Begin each morning with a private sunrise yoga session on the lanai, spend your days snorkeling Molokini Crater, driving the breathtaking Road to Hana, and watching for humpback whales off the coast of Lahaina. Evenings are reserved for candlelit dinners at award-winning restaurants and oceanfront stargazing.

Destination

Wailea, Maui — an upscale coastal resort community known for its calm waters, golden beaches, and world-class golf courses.

Hotel Accommodations

Fairmont Kea Lani Maui — 5 nights, all-suite accommodation with private lanai and ocean views. Recipient of multiple Forbes Travel Guide awards for luxury and service.

Rental Car

Enterprise Rent-A-Car — Convertible Mustang for 5 days — the perfect vehicle for the iconic Road to Hana drive.

From $3,799 per couple

Island Hopper — 10 Nights

For the truly adventurous, the Island Hopper is Hawaii at its fullest. Spend your first three nights on Oahu exploring Waikiki and Pearl Harbor, then island-hop to Maui for three nights of Road to Hana and Haleakala volcano views, and wrap up with four unforgettable nights on the Big Island — home to active volcanoes, black sand beaches, manta ray night dives, and the clearest stargazing skies in the Northern Hemisphere atop Mauna Kea.

Destination

Three Islands — Oahu, Maui, and the Big Island — giving you a complete cross-section of the Hawaiian archipelago's diverse landscapes and cultures.

Hotel Accommodations

Waikiki Beach Marriott Resort (3 nights, Oahu) • Andaz Maui at Wailea Resort (3 nights, Maui) • Hilton Waikoloa Village (4 nights, Big Island)

Rental Car

Budget Car Rental — One mid-size SUV on each island (pick up/drop off at each airport), giving you the freedom to explore at your own pace on all three islands.

From $5,299 per person
