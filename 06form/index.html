Welcome to The Rustic Fork

Nestled in the heart of downtown, The Rustic Fork is your neighborhood destination for hearty, farm-to-table comfort food. We source our ingredients from local farms every week and prepare everything fresh from scratch.

Whether you're stopping in for a quick lunch, treating the family to dinner, or ordering ahead for pickup — we've got you covered with generous portions and honest flavors that keep our guests coming back.

Why Guests Love Us

  • Ingredients sourced within 100 miles of our kitchen
  • Rotating seasonal menu — something new every visit
  • Online ordering with easy pickup and local delivery
  • Friendly, welcoming service for the whole family
  • Gluten-free and vegetarian options always available
